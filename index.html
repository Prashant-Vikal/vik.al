03 / Point of view

Design quality is an architecture problem before it is a taste problem. Good products scale because of good structure, clear decision rights, rigid component logic, and tooling that removes friction from the pipeline. Increasingly, that structure requires AI. I architect systems that compute and recommend, drastically reducing time-to-market while keeping human judgment at the final gate.

04 / Selected work Five systems

01 Methodology Framework

VikSchema Studio

A way to build design systems organized by responsibility, not complexity, so they scale without fragmenting as products and teams grow.

02 Decision quality

Throughline

A hiring evaluation system that makes the reasoning behind every decision visible and keeps the final call human. It computes and recommends. It never auto-decides.

03 Tooling

04 Enablement

VikSense

A custom GPT that converts a design organization's tacit knowledge into an automated onboarding and first-pass review agent. It eliminates the scaling bottleneck of expertise trapped in senior leaders' heads.
